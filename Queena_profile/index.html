ABOUT

Research, interaction, and the ability to make it real

I’m Queena Zheng, a New York–based product designer with a background in product design, design informatics, and interactive media. My work connects research and product structure with hands-on prototyping—from web interfaces to physical installations.

I’m interested in how people understand intelligent systems: what the system does, where human judgment belongs, and how the interaction stays understandable when technology becomes uncertain or unfamiliar.

Selected focus +
UX research, product strategy, prototyping, calm interaction design, and cross-disciplinary storytelling.
